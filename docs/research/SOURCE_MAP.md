## What to pay for

No new subscription is needed to start the public-report collection. The detailed [access audit](DATA_ACCESS.md) records published prices and product limits. Complete individual histories remain an unpriced gap, rather than a promised feature of a cheap subscription.

Koyfin Plus is advertised at USD 39/month billed annually, but its official documentation excludes API access and restricts estimates/valuation downloads. It can be a manual reference tool; buying it would not supply our application feed. [Pricing](https://www.koyfin.com/pricing-llm-info/), [API](https://www.koyfin.com/help/faq/can-i-get-the-data-via-api/), [downloads](https://www.koyfin.com/help/faq/can-i-download-data/).

FactSet and LSEG are candidates if full historical analyst data becomes necessary. No applicable quote or account entitlement is verified. SemiAnalysis can supply industry-model research under separate institutional terms; its newsletter does not include those models. [FactSet estimates](https://insight.factset.com/resources/factset-consensus-estimates-datafeed), [LSEG estimate analytics](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-catalogue/company-data/ibes-estimates/estimate-analytics), [SemiAnalysis Memory Model](https://semianalysis.com/memory-model/).

## Next work package

Build two small historical collections in parallel as implementation work becomes appropriate: Broadcom from BOCOM, and SK hynix from the Korean brokers. Broadcom has an accessible resolved forecast example; Hynix offers several distinct brokers with public numerical models. This is a choice based on data availability, not an investment recommendation.

Choose a fixed historical period and collect every retrievable report, recording gaps. Extract report dates, original model dates, target periods, units and definitions. Match forecasts to comparable actuals and the consensus available at the same horizon. Begin with revenue where EPS normalization is unresolved. Only then calculate forecast errors and decide whether a candidate deserves weight beyond consensus.

Keep all eight companies in the watchlist. Obtain one current, clearly attributable forecast and one matching current consensus for each before presenting a live valuation screen. Defer daily historical bands wherever the necessary forecast history is missing.
