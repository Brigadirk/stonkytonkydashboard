## Why September leads did not refresh the model

The [September 7, 2026 original](https://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260907073604796K_02.pdf) is Soowook Hwang’s investment-strategy note about Astra and AI demand. It has no annual company EPS table and is not Sunwoo Kim’s model. The public analyst-channel September search also returns Overnight Tech news/spot-price summaries and buyback-trading reports. Those do not establish new earnings forecasts. This is a finding about the inspected leads, not a claim that no newer model exists anywhere. The existing Mirae September 7 company models remain separate, newer alternatives by Young-gun Kim.

The [August 19 Hynix capital-return note](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260819180556880K_02.pdf), [March 25 ADR note](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260325115510983K_02.pdf), and [May 6 GDR note](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260505173705844K_02.pdf) contain no annual EPS model. They remain supporting originals without forecast rows.

The [May 26, 2025 Computex excerpt](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20250526074256187K_02.pdf), physical p6, repeats the already collected April 25 EPS vector exactly: FY2025/26/27 43,086.3 / 45,491.2 / 39,174.1. It does not get a fresh model date. Likewise, Hynix in the June 12, 2024 sector report repeats June 10’s model; only the distinct Samsung model is added.

## EPS compatibility: still a real limit

No new cross-broker equivalence is proven. The original parent-attributable EPS label is preserved using the existing Meritz scoped basis. It is not converted to statutory basic or diluted EPS. `compatibility_review.json` records exact source hashes, physical pages, values and forbidden inferences; `basis_alias_recommendations.json` is deliberately empty.

There is positive evidence against blanket equivalence. Meritz’s [January 31, 2025 Samsung original](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20250131211320144K_02.pdf), p1, prints completed FY2024 parent profit of KRW33.6tn and provisional EPS 4,950. The [July 30, 2026 original](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260730201736431K_02.pdf), p8, prints the same rounded FY2024 parent profit with EPS **5,632**. Existing KB/Mirae originals print **4,950**. Therefore even matching parent-profit labels and completed-year numerators do not establish matching share denominators. Historical EPS columns are not imported as issuer outcomes.

For Hynix, dividing each broker’s explicitly printed FY2026 parent profit by EPS implies approximately 719.33m shares for Meritz July 29, 712.70m for KB May 29, and 722.63m for Mirae September 7. These are diagnostic calculations from rounded reported model values, not new EPS estimates. Different share-count forecasts could be legitimate, but the public tables do not define one common forward weighted-average/basic/diluted policy. The [July 27 Meritz sector discussion](http://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/research/WorkFlow/20260726181944311K_02.pdf), physical p68, says EPS incorporates nonoperating value and dilution effects. That statement still does not define its table as statutory diluted EPS.

A compatible multi-analyst EPS ensemble remains blocked on denominator and adjustment evidence, not on the absence of additional named analysts. A future collection could gather broker methodology or explicit model share schedules. Revenue estimates have a simpler common unit but cannot substitute for EPS in earnings valuation bands.

## Dating, attribution and reproduction

Source discovery used the analyst team’s public [Meritz Tech channel](https://t.me/s/merITz_tech), including searches for exact company tickers and report announcements; final evidence is the original broker-hosted PDF. Discovery HTML and post text are retained under `discovery/`. The currently displayed Telegram post timestamps are discovery metadata only: they do not prove that linked PDF bytes were available unchanged at those historical times. Original availability remains unverified, with no fabricated timestamp.

Printed dates govern observations. Image-only sector covers for September 12, 2025 and July 27, 2026 were rendered and visually verified; their text-extraction date would otherwise select an unrelated date later in the file. The November 11, 2021, February 15, 2024 and June 12, 2024 dates are on physical p2. For July 27, the Samsung and Hynix annual summaries are individually attributable to Sunwoo’s company-section bylines on physical p63 and p68 respectively, although the entire report has several authors.

`observations.csv` follows the established Korean collection schema. Annual columns explicitly marked E are used; preliminary completed-year P columns, target prices, consensus charts, operating-profit commentary and undated revision figures are excluded. Revenue `value` is normalized to base KRW; `original_value` and `original_unit` retain the printed trillion/billion units. Model date follows the current report date where no distinct earlier model date is established; unchanged reprints identified above are excluded rather than made artificially fresh.

Run `python3 data/collection/korea_round8/restore_sources.py` to verify originals and restore only missing files from the exact public URLs in `source_recipe.json`; changed remote bytes fail the retained SHA-256 check. Run `python3 data/collection/korea_round8/extract_reviewed.py` to reproduce CSVs and validation offline. `collect_sources.py` records the initial bounded channel-lead retrieval process. Hashes, all fiscal vectors, source labels, source dates and company-section attribution are checked. Critical changed-layout summaries and sector tables/covers were visually reviewed; PNGs are retained under `review/`.

No subscription, proxy credits, paid account or external messaging was needed. No Grok tool was callable in this agent session; public primary sources supplied the additional data. Five-year history remains irregular, particularly in 2022–2023, rather than a complete daily analyst-estimate database. No shared scripts, merged datasets, existing sources or UI files were edited.
