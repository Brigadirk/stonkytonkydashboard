# U.S. forecast collection, round 4

Collected on 10 September 2026 for Broadcom, Alphabet, NVIDIA and Micron. This round retained **72 new original broker PDFs** from 122 bounded public-URL checks and extracted **558 annual observations, including 324 EPS observations**. Eighteen observations remain quarantined. Two retained BOCOM morning reports have no identified target-company model and are excluded from relevant-forecast coverage. The full Chinese Broadcom model is retained as corroboration, without counting its more precise figures as additional forecasts alongside the English summary.

The main discovery was that Firstrade's public Morningstar archive contains substantially more reports than search-engine results expose. Checks around earnings dates and dates cited in the reports' own analyst-note histories returned originals for 2021–2026. A filename was only a discovery hint: the report timestamp, numerical model date, author and values were read from the downloaded PDF. No login, proxy, ScrapingBee credit or market subscription was used.

## Fiscal labels and accounting definitions

Several older Morningstar summary tables have shifted fiscal labels. Their literal labels remain in `source_period_label`; `fiscal_period` contains the documented issuer-year mapping. This is a correction of period identity, not an inferred EPS value.

- **NVIDIA February 2022 report:** the summary's two historical columns show rounded USD17bn and USD27bn, which are FY2021 and FY2022. The printed forecast-year labels require **+1 year**. The issuer's release identifies FY2022 revenue as USD26.914bn and FY2021 as USD16.675bn. [Morningstar original](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2022/0P000003RE_20220216_RT.pdf), [SEC-filed issuer release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581022000008/q4fy22pr.htm).
- **NVIDIA February, May, August and November 2023 reports:** two historical revenues of USD26,914m and USD26,974m identify FY2022 and FY2023, so their forecast columns also require **+1 year**. The August prose explicitly calls the USD54bn forecast FY2024, ending January 2024. The February 2024 report reprints the November model's exact revenue/EPS vector with corrected fiscal labels, independently corroborating the mapping; that reprint is not a new numerical model. [Issuer FY2023 release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2023/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2023/), [August original, model on page 17](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2023/0P000003RE_20230823_RT.pdf), [February 2024 reprint](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000003RE_20240221_RT.pdf).
- **Alphabet February 2023 report:** its October 2022 model puts USD182,527m and USD257,637m under labels 2021 and 2022. The same physical page's historical financial table identifies those as 2020 and 2021. Forecast labels require **−1 year**. [Original, page 16](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2023/0P000002HD_20230202_RT.pdf).
- **Micron December 2021 and March 2023 reports:** their older models similarly require **−1 year**. Revenue/EPS pairs in the preceding historical financials identify the years: December's rounded USD23bn/21bn are FY2019/FY2020; March's USD21,435m/27,705m are FY2020/FY2021. Periods already complete when the reprint appeared must not serve as the next fiscal year. [December original](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2021/0P000003MC_20211220_RT.pdf), [March original](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2023/0P000003MC_20230328_RT.pdf).

Micron June and December 2024 tables have an October month-end header, although their historical columns identify the correct issuer fiscal years. Alphabet's July 2024 table has a March header but historical revenues of USD282,836m and USD307,394m identify calendar 2022 and 2023. The year columns are retained; the verified issuer fiscal calendar supplies actual period ends. These header errors are disclosed in every affected row. [Micron June](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000003MC_20240626_RT.pdf), [Micron December](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000003MC_20241218_RT.pdf), [Alphabet July](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000002HD_20240723_RT.pdf).

Older tables labeled simply “Diluted Earnings Per Share” remain under `diluted_adjustment_basis_unresolved`. For semiconductor companies their historical values often match adjusted rather than reported earnings, while Alphabet's often match reported earnings. They should not be silently merged with the modern, explicitly separate reported and adjusted rows.

NVIDIA changed its own non-GAAP definition to include stock-based compensation from FY2027 Q1. The 2026 Morningstar tables do not separately document their treatment of this change. Their printed adjusted EPS is retained, with a comparability note; an invariant accounting basis across vintages has not been established. [NVIDIA's official announcement and reconciliation](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/).

## Attribution, split bases and exclusions

The observed Morningstar authors change over time: Abhinav Davuluri to William Kerwin for Broadcom/Micron; Davuluri to Brian Colello for NVIDIA; and Ali Mogharabi through Michael Hodel to Malik Ahmed Khan for Alphabet. The individual names remain attached to each source. The June 2023 Broadcom original has a Kerwin analyst note and Colello valuation discussion around the model date, so six rows remain quarantined for model-author review. The December 2021 Broadcom model and its March 2022 reprint contribute twelve further quarantined rows because their historical-year/EPS vector is inconsistent. [Broadcom handoff report](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2023/0P0000KU35_20230602_RT.pdf), [December 2021 inconsistent model](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2021/0P0000KU35_20211209_RT.pdf).

EPS is retained on its printed split basis. No extracted Morningstar model/report interval crosses the relevant known split dates. The change from pre-split to post-split values is visible in the actual EPS columns: NVIDIA's FY2024 non-GAAP EPS is about 12.96 before its June 2024 split and 1.30 afterward; Broadcom FY2023 EPS is about 42.25 before its July split and 4.22 afterward. Alphabet's July 2022 table partially restates historical EPS; forecast values are already on the post-split basis. The `share_basis_date` field does not claim an independent availability timestamp. [NVIDIA before](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000003RE_20240522_RT.pdf), [NVIDIA after](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P000003RE_20240828_RT.pdf), [Broadcom before](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P0000KU35_20240612_RT.pdf), [Broadcom after](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2024/0P0000KU35_20240905_RT.pdf), [Alphabet after](https://invest.firstrade.com/ms/equity_reports/sr/2022/0P000002HD_20220727_RT.pdf).
