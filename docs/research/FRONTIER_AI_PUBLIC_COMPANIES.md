# Publicly traded companies in frontier AI

Research date: 2026-09-11. Scope: global public equities.

This is a company research list. It is not a framework result, a human thesis,
or a trade decision. No share-price valuation or expected return was calculated.

Here, **frontier AI** means advanced general-purpose models and the systems
needed to train and run them. A company qualifies through model development,
large-scale compute services, or a material role in the supply chain. Business
software companies that apply these models have a separate group. Inclusion
does not establish that a company's models lead all current benchmarks.

Company products and listings below come from issuer or exchange sources.
The choice of companies, their importance, and the questions for further
research are analyst judgments. This is a selected list, not a complete index.
Sources were checked on the research date. Undated product pages can change.
Product announcements and planned deployments do not prove delivered revenue.

## How to use this list

- Most direct listed model exposure in this selection: Alphabet, Meta, SpaceX through xAI, Alibaba, Z.ai, and MiniMax. Microsoft, Amazon, Tencent, and Baidu also develop models, but inclusion does not claim equal performance across tasks.
- Cloud and model distribution overlap hardware and software. Keep one company record even when it has several roles.
- GOOGL and GOOG are two share classes of one business. A US depositary receipt and Hong Kong listing are also not two separate companies.
- Exchange access, share classes, and depositary receipt terms need checking before a later investment decision. This list does not select a trading venue for Edwin.
- The list is selective. It does not establish the current private or public status of companies omitted from it.

## Scope and next research step

The complete list has 33 companies: 12 core hardware suppliers, 4 further chip
and design companies, 2 dedicated AI cloud operators, 12 model/platform and
deployment companies, and 3 adjacent business software companies.

For a smaller first study, start with NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, TSMC, ASML,
SK hynix, Micron, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Alibaba. This is a
judgment about coverage of the industry, not expected share returns. Add
Cerebras, Z.ai, MiniMax, and SpaceX when studying alternative processors or
more direct model exposure. Use the separate company questions above to
select the next financial analysis.

The next step is to check AI revenue, margins, cash needs, competitive
position, and valuation company by company. A large role in AI does not
by itself establish an attractive share price.
