# Earnings forecaster source audit: Broadcom, Alphabet, Nvidia and ASML

Research cutoff: 10 September 2026. This is a bounded source audit, not a ranking of forecast accuracy. No subscription, login, provider contact or purchase was used. Prices below are advertised research subscription prices, not verified data-feed licences.

## What we can start with

The most useful immediately accessible numerical sources found were Dawei Wang's BOCOM International reports for Broadcom and Nvidia, Malik Ahmed Khan's Morningstar model for Alphabet, and Javier Correonero's Morningstar work on ASML. These have explicit dated earnings or revenue estimates. The established bank analysts listed below are candidates to investigate once their forecast histories can be obtained. Being on an issuer's coverage list does not establish forecasting skill.

Several historical estimates are retrievable without paying. None of the sources audited provides a verified complete daily history of each candidate's earnings forecasts. Historical samples must not be displayed as their current September 2026 forecasts. “Not found” below means not retrieved in this audit, not that the information does not exist.

An initial outcome can be matched. BOCOM's 18 November FY2025 estimate was USD 63.35 billion revenue and USD 6.84 non-GAAP diluted EPS. Broadcom subsequently reported USD 63.887 billion and USD 6.82 on 11 December 2025. That is roughly 0.84% below actual revenue and 0.29% above actual EPS. This was a forecast after the fiscal year had ended and only 23 days before release, not a 90-day or 180-day forecast. No contemporaneous consensus comparison has been completed, so this single close estimate cannot establish superior skill. [BOCOM forecast](https://files.bocomgroup.com/download/mexp-251118e.pdf), [Broadcom FY2025 release](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025).

The DBS URL is not a stable dated archive. Search indexing exposed an older version with Visible Alpha-labelled tables, while the retrieved document displayed 18 June 2026 and Company/DBS-labelled tables. It also labels the fiscal year “FY Apr” and carries older forward-looking commentary. Treat each retrieved version separately, preserve the stated source, and verify the fiscal period before using it. Do not assume every number printed in a broker report is that broker's own estimate. [DBS PDF](https://www.dbs.com/content/article/pdf/US_clover/Broadcom.pdf).

## Paid sources to consider only after this audit

- **Morningstar Investor:** advertised US website price USD 249/year or USD 34.95/month. Some useful model tables and articles are already public. We have not verified that an Investor subscription includes downloadable point-in-time forecast histories, API use, or every original model vintage. [Official price page](https://www.morningstar.com/mm/).
- **SemiAnalysis:** a relevant additional research candidate for Broadcom, Nvidia and the memory supply chain. Its [model description](https://semianalysis.com/models-research/) explicitly covers company/SKU shipment, price and revenue forecasts, including Nvidia and Broadcom. The individual newsletter is advertised at USD 500/year, but **institutional models are excluded**. Institutional model pricing and historical-vintage availability are unknown. [Subscription page](https://semianalysis.com/subscribe-to-semianalysis/), [FAQ](https://semianalysis.com/faq/). Do not buy the newsletter assuming it supplies the earnings-model feed.
- **BOCOM/DBS:** sampled public PDFs cost nothing to retrieve in this audit. That does not establish a free complete archive, automated feed, or redistribution entitlement. Both need source/version checks before regular ingestion.
- **Named institutional bank analysts:** no retail subscription price or complete forecast panel verified here. A licensed estimates feed may be a better eventual route than collecting reports one firm at a time, but provider coverage and entitlement must be demonstrated with a sample.

## Recommended first work package

1. Build the first historical extraction exercise around BOCOM Broadcom. It has multiple original dated reports and one already-resolved annual outcome. Keep the March revenue figures quarantined.
2. Add BOCOM Nvidia and the Morningstar Alphabet table to exercise fiscal-year mapping, accounting-basis fields, and model-date versus publication-date handling.
3. Obtain one current consensus and one current named-analyst forecast for each company before presenting a current valuation screen. This audit's old samples are for source validation.
4. For accuracy scoring, assemble all retrievable forecasts across a predefined run of earnings releases, including misses and unchanged forecasts. Match 90-day and 180-day horizons to the contemporaneous consensus and comparable actuals. Until then, mark every named candidate “unranked; insufficient evaluated history.”

The sources above support beginning a manual pilot now. They do not yet support the proposed full historical forward-multiple bands or a defensible “best analyst” score.
