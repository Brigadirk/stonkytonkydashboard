# Five-year memory analyst evidence audit

Research cutoff: **2026-09-10**. Requested observation window: **2021-09-10 through 2026-09-10**. Instruments: SK Hynix 000660 KRX, Samsung Electronics 005930 KRX, Micron MU Nasdaq and the current Sandisk SNDK Nasdaq. This extends [the initial availability audit](Memory_Forecaster_Audit.md).

## Decision supported by the evidence

There is enough evidence to choose a serious Korean comparison set, but not enough to name the best five-year earnings forecaster for any of these four stocks. No public, stock-specific five-year EPS-accuracy scores were verified in this bounded audit. Public PDFs establish forecasts; sector awards can support candidate selection; neither substitutes for matching forecasts to results.

For **SK Hynix and Samsung**, add **Sunwoo Kim at Meritz** and **Roh Geun-chang at Hyundai Motor Securities** to the first shortlist. They have relevant quantitative sector-award evidence, which is a stronger selection reason than merely publishing accessible research. Retain **Young-gun Kim at Mirae Asset** and **Jeff Kim at KB** as competing forecasters with accessible archives. For **Micron**, investigate **C. J. Muse, Vijay Rakesh, Matt Bryson and William Kerwin**, with no accuracy ordering yet. For **Sandisk**, begin with the same four, but only score the current standalone company from 2025 onward. Sources and limits follow.

### StarMine findings and exclusions

The live official **StarMine Forecaster Awards** page found during this research concerns accuracy in **Reuters economic and market polls**, including economics, foreign exchange, rates, indices and commodities. It is not a current equity EPS-estimator league table. Do not present one of those winners as the best Micron or Samsung earnings analyst. [LSEG award description](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/starmine-forecaster-awards)

LSEG's **StarMine SmartEstimates** product does use historical analyst accuracy and estimate recency to weight forecasts for a company and fiscal period. Its public product page does not disclose the requested individual memory-stock rankings. **StarMine Monitor** distinguishes earnings-estimate accuracy from stock-picking returns. Those are the relevant capabilities to investigate in a licensed sample, rather than assuming a general research award or price-target success rate answers the question. [SmartEstimates description](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-catalogue/analytics/quantitative-analytics/starmine-smartestimates), [StarMine Monitor](https://monitor.starmine.com/)

Searches for 2021–2026 StarMine best earnings estimators connected to these companies and shortlisted names did not establish a relevant public winner/score series. Several results were recent biographies referring to much older awards. Neither those older awards nor stock-picking rankings were converted into present EPS-accuracy evidence. This is a finding about the retrieved evidence, not proof that licensed scores do not exist.

## How to turn this shortlist into an accuracy result

The following is a proposed measurement design, not a claim about an existing vendor methodology.

1. **Enumerate before scoring.** Collect all obtainable reports for each candidate and target company across the window. Record expected publication opportunities, observed reports and gaps. Avoid choosing memorable wins or only today's surviving analysts.
2. **Keep point-in-time identity and provenance.** Each row needs instrument/entity, analyst ID, firm at publication, coauthors, printed date, original availability timestamp where obtainable, URL, page, retrieval time, file hash, target period and metric basis. A PDF downloaded today does not by itself prove it was available unchanged on its printed date.
3. **Match a common horizon.** Follow the central [evaluation rules](../ANALYST_EVALUATION.md): quarterly forecasts at 90- and 180-day pre-release cutoffs, annual forecasts at 180 and 365 days, each scored separately. Record forecast age. At each cutoff, admit only data already public then, including any awards used to choose analysts. The 2026 award must not influence a simulated 2022 analyst selection.
4. **Reconcile actuals.** Match consolidated revenue to consolidated revenue, parent profit to parent profit, and EPS to the same accounting and share-count definition. Keep management guidance, broker forecasts and report-embedded consensus as different source types. Record first-reported actuals separately from later restatements.
5. **Report errors and sample sizes together.** Begin with absolute revenue error and basis-matched EPS error, each relative to a contemporaneous consensus under the same horizon. For loss-making/near-zero EPS periods, avoid percentage errors with unstable denominators. Report bias, median error, number of fiscal outcomes and year-by-year consistency before assigning weights.
6. **Require held-out evidence.** Choose or weight analysts using earlier years, then evaluate the subsequent period. Five annual outcomes are a small sample; quarterly results add observations but do not eliminate dependence within a memory cycle. Keep a simple consensus baseline in every comparison.

The first practical test is the October 2021 Hynix paired vintage above, followed by broader report enumeration for Meritz, Hyundai, Mirae and KB. For US names, obtain a sample of licensed individual forecasts only if the public archive cannot support the needed period. No provider was contacted, no subscription price was assumed, and no paid access was obtained in this audit.
