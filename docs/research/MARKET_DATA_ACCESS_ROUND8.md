# Historical estimates access check

Checked 10 September 2026. Public source descriptions establish product categories, not a quote or confirmed entitlement for this ten-stock project. Retained page/PDF downloads and retrieval results are in `data/discovery/access_round8/`.

## What the remaining historical dataset requires

The required object is an individual broker/analyst EPS revision with its fiscal target, earnings definition, share convention and original availability. A historical consensus line does not expose its historical members. Today's consensus plus earlier share prices cannot reconstruct the old denominator. Initial issuer outcomes and any provider-comparable actual must keep their different definitions.

FactSet describes historical detail estimates tied to individual analysts and brokers and distinguishes company actuals from broker actuals. Its OnDemand manual also documents historical broker snapshots and detail history. These are relevant product capabilities, but exact coverage for AVGO, GOOGL, NVDA, SK hynix, Samsung, MU, current SNDK, Amsterdam ASML, AAPL and BESI, export/storage entitlement and price remain unconfirmed. [FactSet datafeed description](https://insight.factset.com/resources/factset-consensus-estimates-datafeed), [OnDemand reference](https://go.factset.com/hubfs/Website/Website_Downloads/Statistical%20Package%20Integration/factset%20ondemand%20web%20services%20reference%20manual_2.0.pdf).

LSEG describes I/B/E/S detail, consensus and comparable actuals. Its quantitative-research brochure distinguishes historical estimates from point-in-time daily historical snapshots and lists different delivery channels. The provider must establish that an offered package includes individual detail and the required timing semantics, not merely daily means or a current-estimates screen. [I/B/E/S product page](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/financial-data/company-data/ibes-estimates), [LSEG quantitative data brochure](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/data-analytics/en_us/documents/brochures/lseg-data-for-quant-research-brochure.pdf).

Neither inspected primary source establishes a minimum purchase price for our exact sample. The smallest *requested* deliverable is a one-time ten-name detail export with matched actuals and historical consensus, subject to the [sample specification](../DATA_SAMPLE_REQUEST.md). A one-time export has not been confirmed as a purchasable package. No institutional subscription is recommended before sample validation.

## ScrapingBee

ScrapingBee supplies browser rendering and proxy-assisted retrieval. Its documentation lists ordinary requests at one credit and JavaScript-rendered requests at five, with more expensive proxy modes. The public pricing page advertises a 1,000-credit trial and a lowest paid Hobby plan at $19/month for 75,000 credits, before VAT, on the date checked. Pricing is not evidence that it can retrieve a particular blocked report. [API documentation](https://www.scrapingbee.com/documentation/), [pricing](https://www.scrapingbee.com/pricing/).

No critical public URL has yet been demonstrated to need ScrapingBee. Local HTTP and browser requests recovered the models added in this pass. Subscription-only tables at IEX and client-only broker research are access entitlements, not solved by proxy credits. Therefore the supported ScrapingBee purchase for this pass is **none**. If a specific public source later fails, reproduce that failure and test the exact URL before selecting credits.
