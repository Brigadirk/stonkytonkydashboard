# U.S. EPS outcomes and forecast-basis evaluation, round 5

Collected on 10 September 2026. This collection supplies **20 original initial annual earnings releases and 35 EPS actuals** for Broadcom, Alphabet, NVIDIA and Micron, FY2021–FY2025. Each company has five GAAP diluted EPS outcomes; the three semiconductor issuers also publish five explicitly defined non-GAAP diluted EPS outcomes. No issuer non-GAAP EPS was identified in these Alphabet releases.

A separate check retained five Morningstar originals around its 2025 table-format transition. Three are new originals; two duplicate previously retained sources and serve as supporting evidence. Their explicit current/prior panels establish **five narrowly scoped accounting-basis aliases** and add **36 annual observations, including 23 EPS observations**, from three previously missing report/model combinations. These outcomes and forecasts support a small fixed-horizon comparison pilot; they do not establish analyst rankings.

## Accounting and share definitions

`reported_diluted` means annual U.S. GAAP diluted EPS in the initial release. `non_gaap_diluted` means the issuer's own explicitly reconciled annual EPS for that release. Every outcome has a release-specific `definition_id`; this prevents a shared broad label from silently asserting an invariant exclusion policy. `issuer_eps_definitions.json` retains the original reconciliation text, with exact items, amounts, signs, diluted-share-count treatment and footnotes. The CSV definition is a concise guide to that particular table.

Non-GAAP denominators also differ. Broadcom's reconciliation adjusts diluted shares for the treatment of future unrecognized stock compensation; Micron separately reconciles its non-GAAP diluted shares, with a zero stock-compensation share adjustment in the FY2023 loss. NVIDIA's table supplies its diluted share count and tax treatment. Consequently an issuer non-GAAP number is not interchangeable with any broker row called adjusted EPS. See the original reconciliation tables linked above and each CSV's `definition_source_locator`.

Split normalization must apply to both forecast and actual using their respective share-basis dates. Broadcom FY2021–FY2023 values precede its July 2024 10-for-1 split; Alphabet FY2021 precedes its July 2022 20-for-1 split; NVIDIA FY2021 precedes both the July 2021 4-for-1 and June 2024 10-for-1 splits, while FY2022–FY2024 precede the latter. The later issuer releases explicitly restate comparative EPS; the first-release values retained here remain unchanged. [Broadcom split announcement](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-second-quarter-fiscal-year-2024-financial), [Alphabet filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000119312522167375/d294315d8k.htm), [NVIDIA 2021 FAQ](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/doc_downloads/doc_faq/06/21/NVIDIA-2021-Stock-Split-FAQ.pdf), [NVIDIA 2024 FAQ](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/doc_downloads/2024/06/nvidia-2024-stock-split_faq_investors.pdf).

`share_basis_date` is the initial release date for these actuals, identifying the printed split epoch. It does not claim an independently proven dissemination timestamp. `actual_available_at` remains blank; initial release dates are known to the day. The `result_version` is always `initial_annual_earnings_release`, so later restatements and changes to comparative columns are not substituted.

## Fixed-horizon pilot and limits

A useful pilot selects the latest eligible original report strictly before a stated cutoff, such as 90, 180 or 365 days before the initial annual release. It preserves individual analyst and publisher identity, compares the same issuer fiscal year and currency, and normalizes both share bases. GAAP-to-GAAP comparisons can use the explicit reported rows and the narrowly proven Alphabet model. Unresolved or merely adjusted rows remain visible as exclusions. Initial outcomes do not justify a rank without enough independent target years and a matched consensus or naive benchmark.

Both printed-report age and numerical-model age should be retained. The new Broadcom report was printed 3 April 2025 but its model dates to 6 March. At the FY2025 180-day cutoff, 14 June 2025, those ages are 72 and 100 days. The app applies its 90-day freshness limit to the numerical model date, so this 100-day-old model is excluded at that horizon. The 72-day printed-report age does not refresh it. The UI and method notes state the model-age rule. Exact historical dissemination remains unverified for every broker original; a printed date is evidence of the document's date, not a separately audited availability log.

After integration with the separately collected Apple outcomes, the root-owned evaluator has 40 issuer EPS outcomes and three comparable pairs: Apple FY2025 at 180 days, Broadcom FY2025 at 90 days, and Alphabet FY2025 at 90 days. The full application dataset contains 850 usable EPS observations. These are integration totals, not the 35-outcome scope of this research. The root-owned evaluator supplies the scores and exclusions; this research provides actuals and scoped matching evidence without a second conflicting ranking implementation. Negative EPS such as Micron FY2023 is preserved; it should not be forced into a positive P/E denominator or hidden by changing its sign.

## Artifacts and reproduction

All additions are under [data/collection/us_evaluation_round5](../../data/collection/us_evaluation_round5).

- `issuer_eps_outcomes.csv`: 35 reviewed actuals. Identity and period fields are `outcome_id`, `company_id`, `fiscal_period`, `fiscal_period_end`. Measurement fields are `metric`, `accounting_basis`, `value`, `currency`, `unit`. Timing fields are `initial_release_date`, `actual_available_at`, `share_basis_date`. Evidence fields are source URL/file/hash, physical page or HTML table locator, exact printed EPS row, definition and definition ID. `result_version`, `validation_status` and notes preserve limits.
- `issuer_manifest.csv`: 20 source records with original URL, local file, format, SHA-256, derived text path, retrieval timestamp, status and `document_kind=issuer_earnings_release`. Its file paths are relative to this collection; outcome paths are repo-relative. HTML has no invented page number: all HTML locators count tables in original document order, starting at one. Alphabet actuals are on physical PDF page 1.
- `issuer_eps_definitions.json`: release-specific definitions and retained exact reconciliation text. `validation.json` records original-hash and annual-current-column checks. `finalize.py` rebuilds these outputs offline and asserts all 35 EPS values against the original annual table rows.
- `manifest.csv`: five compatible broker-source records; `observations.csv`: 36 new annual model observations. Two already collected source copies are supporting evidence only. `basis_alias_recommendations.json` supplies five model-and-period scopes; it supports 16 original EPS rows, including the duplicated NVIDIA November-model reprint.
- `basis_bridge/attempts.csv`: 14 bounded public URL candidates; five originals recovered. Initial transient TLS failures were retried once without bypassing certificate verification. `reference_forecasts.csv` freezes the relevant pre-integration rows, each linked to original source/hash/page. `finalize_bridge.py` reproduces the broker observations, source manifest and alias evidence offline, independent of subsequent shared dataset changes.

Validation checked retained hashes, annual versus quarterly columns, current versus prior years, loss signs, unique IDs, exact forecast/revenue vectors, explicit model-date scopes and physical page references. No paid subscription, scraping credits, provider contact or external messages were used.
