Research cutoff: **10 September 2026**. Intended evaluation window: **10 September 2021–10 September 2026**. This bounded audit uses public primary sources. No subscription, login, purchase or provider contact was used.

## Finding

**We have a credible institutional candidate list, but have not established who predicted these four companies' earnings best over five years.** No complete public analyst-by-company panel, or named stock-specific earnings-accuracy ranking covering the requested period, was retrieved. Every name below remains **unranked for earnings accuracy**. Coverage, research reputation, stock-recommendation performance and earnings-estimate accuracy are separate evidence categories.

The earlier [forecaster source audit](US_ASML_Forecaster_Audit.md) prioritizes reports that are easy to retrieve. For the user's expanded five-year question, use the institutional cohort below first. Public BOCOM and Morningstar samples remain useful for validating extraction and accounting fields; accessibility does not establish forecasting superiority or five years of coverage.

## What the relevant accuracy evidence actually says

**LSEG has the relevant type of measurement.** Its SmartEstimates documentation says analyst accuracy is evaluated for the relevant company and fiscal period; the model also accounts for estimate recency and filters stale clusters and outliers. It advertises history from 1998. Those statements support investigating the underlying data, but do not prove our access to named analyst identities, all historical vintages or every requested stock. [LSEG SmartEstimates](https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/financial-data/analytics/quantitative-analytics/starmine-smartestimates).

**There is a concrete Nvidia example within the requested window.** LSEG's article dated **18 November 2025**, before the next day's earnings release, reports two five-star analysts with FY2027 “Bold” estimates up to 22% above consensus. It does **not** identify the analysts or give their individual scoring histories. We therefore cannot map that evidence to Rasgon, Arya, Lipacis, Moore or any other named candidate. The same article gives these contemporaneous figures:

| Metric for the imminent Nvidia quarter | SmartEstimate | Consensus | Limitation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| EPS, USD/share | 1.25 | 1.24 | Accounting/dilution basis is not defined in the article. |
| Revenue, USD billion | 55.1 | 54.8 | One aggregate forecast vintage. |

The article also reports eight recent EPS estimates ranging from USD 1.26 to USD 1.32, without attribution. These are useful baseline snapshots, not eight identified forecasters or a five-year evaluation. Its ARM score measures analyst revisions and must not be relabelled as an analyst's EPS-accuracy score. [LSEG Nvidia Spotlight, 18 November 2025](https://lipperalpha.refinitiv.com/2025/11/starmine-spotlight-002-starmine-flags-strong-analyst-sentiment-ahead-of-nvidia-earnings/).

**The public StarMine Monitor front door requires login.** It describes analyst performance tools covering earnings estimates and recommendations, but did not expose the requested named historical panel without authenticated access. Access price and permissions were not verified. [StarMine Monitor](https://monitor.starmine.com/).

These examples explain why five years of history must be keyed to a person and their firm at the forecast date, rather than simply to today's broker name.

- **Mark Lipacis:** Evercore announced his hire from Jefferies on **3 April 2024**. Request his Jefferies history before the move and Evercore history after it. The announcement establishes the firm transition, not exact initiation dates for each company. [Official announcement](https://investors.evercore.com/node/22046/pdf).
- **Mark Mahaney:** Evercore's 2021 shareholder letter says he joined in **March 2021**, before this window. His biography lists previous firms including RBC. Do not mix old RBC forecasts into an Evercore-only series, or assume today's coverage proves every month of coverage. The transcript alone does not establish the exact transition date or Hari's prior estimate history. ## Retrieval specification for the next step

Obtain a small demonstrable sample of the licensed analyst-level estimates history before choosing a subscription. The sample must allow an analyst's original forecasts to be matched to comparable actuals and the consensus that existed on the same date. Required fields are company/security identity, analyst identity, contributing firm, publication/availability timestamp, forecast metric, target fiscal period, value, currency, accounting/dilution basis, revisions/withdrawals and comparable actuals. Verify whether the provider exposes individual analyst identities or only brokers.

For all four companies, request the full historical contributor set from September 2021, including departed analysts and suspended coverage. A current shortlist helps inspect samples but should not define the final population. Rank each company's revenue and EPS forecasting separately at fixed horizons, compare against contemporaneous consensus, record resolved-period counts, and retain short-history analysts as a separate cohort. A five-year interval supplies only about 20 quarterly outcomes; many revisions to the same quarter are not additional independent predictions.

Only outcomes reported by 10 September 2026 can contribute to an evaluation frozen at that date. Future-period forecasts can inform valuation views but cannot earn accuracy credit yet. If the intended historical trading test chooses analysts using their later 2026 performance, it introduces hindsight: selection must use only performance already observable at each simulated date.

**Deliverable status:** institutional retrieval cohort and dated source map complete; five-year named earnings-accuracy ranking remains dependent on obtaining and evaluating the historical estimate panel. No analyst should be displayed as “best” on the evidence in this audit.
