## Definition and timestamp decisions

- **Three separate EUR EPS series:** IEX own forecasts, StockWatch/LSEG consensus, and Eugene/Bloomberg consensus. Their EPS adjustment and basic-versus-diluted denominators are unspecified. The collection records that limitation in the basis fields and does not alias them to each other, to Morningstar, or to issuer reported diluted EPS. Matching actual EPS values alone would not establish equivalent forecast definitions.
- **April 2026 IEX author identity:** the disclosure prints the surname Laman; the two retained October 2025 IEX articles explicitly name Hildo Laman. The April name is normalized on that same-publisher identity evidence, which is recorded in the row notes. Its annual table is the writer's own revised model; a separate quarterly comparison image uses Bloomberg consensus and is not extracted here.
- **April 2026 FY2025 exclusion:** the annual table still labels FY2025 as an estimate, but its €591.3m revenue and €1.66 EPS are already released FY2025 actuals. Those cells are excluded from forecast observations. The FY2026–28 model is explicitly raised in response to the current outlook, supporting a contemporaneous 23 April model revision. This is not a separately printed financial-model timestamp.
- **October 2025 timing:** the first article's publication notice says 6 October; its Saxo URL contains 7 October. The original publication date is retained, and separate redistribution timing remains unverified. The author explicitly leaves his numerical forecasts unchanged; their earlier origin is unknown, so no model/revision date is assigned. The 23 October article changes FY2027 and adds FY2028, but has no separately printed model timestamp.
- **Consensus timing:** dated publication establishes a captured dated table, not the exact time each contributing analyst revised their estimate. No separate consensus model date is invented. All independent historical-availability timestamp fields remain blank. These sources support the app's disclosed publication-date assumptions, not strict timestamp-verified backtesting.
- **Units and periods:** annual figures are transcribed directly; neither EPS-from-PE arithmetic nor FX conversion is used. EUR per-share wording is explicit in the source tables or, for April IEX, its surrounding forecast explanation. Eugene explicitly states December year-end. Fiscal-calendar and split normalization remains with the root integration workstream.

## Reproduction and checks

`python3 data/collection/besi_brokers/extract.py` rebuilds the compatible `manifest.csv` and `observations.csv`. It checks every retained source SHA-256, compares all extracted Saxo EPS/revenue values against the corresponding fiscal-labelled HTML cells, and checks the Eugene page's period/currency/consensus evidence. Eugene physical page 154 and the Kasikorn image were visually reviewed; retained visual evidence is in `evidence/`. The discovery JSONs retain URLs and retrieval timestamps. The 13-row manifest covers original numerical sources and reviewed exclusions; `extraction_summary.json` records counts. No shared export or app file is written by this extractor.

Forecast accuracy remains unscored. Completed fiscal periods alone do not reconcile unresolved EPS definitions to issuer actuals. There are no definition aliases proposed in this collection.
